PageShooter — Privacy Policy

PageShooter respects your privacy and collects only the minimum data needed to run a match:

  • Guest Sessions: Online mode generates an ephemeral random session ID and display name stored in an HttpOnly browser-session cookie.
  • Local Pass & Play: Offline match progress is saved locally in your browser's IndexedDB and never leaves your device.
  • No Third-Party Tracking: PageShooter does not sell user data or run advertising trackers.
